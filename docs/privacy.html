PRIVACY POLICY

Luthifer — luthier helper tools
Effective October 19, 2026

The short version

Luthifer does not collect, send or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

To remember your work between sessions, Luthifer saves the following only on your device, in the app's local storage:

  • the nut width, offsets, number of strings and string gauges you last entered;
  • your chosen units and display settings;
  • string sets you save.

This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

The website version

The web version of Luthifer works the same way: your inputs and saved string sets are kept in your browser's local storage on your own device and are never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser's data for this site deletes them.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver the pages; see GitHub's privacy statement. Luthifer itself adds no cookies, analytics or tracking.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No network requests from the mobile app: it works fully offline.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone.

Children

Luthifer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page.
